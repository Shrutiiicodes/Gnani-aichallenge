Why

Banks, insurers and edtechs record training videos in English, but their customers and frontline staff speak 10+ languages. Studio dubbing takes weeks.

How

Gnani Prisma transcribes, Gnani Voice Clone captures the speaker, an LLM adapts the script the way Indians actually talk, and the dub is voiced and synced back onto the video.

Languages

Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi, Hinglish and English.
